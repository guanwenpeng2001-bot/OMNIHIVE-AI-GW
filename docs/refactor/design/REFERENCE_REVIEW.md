## Consolidated decisions

1. Amber is the only brand/action accent. Semantic success/error colors communicate status only. Neutral charcoal and warm white carry structure.
2. Model discovery combines OpenRouter facets, Fireworks summary density and Vercel endpoint attributes. Permission, price and capability are separate facts.
3. Console surfaces use 1px boundaries, modest radii, restrained shadows, compact mono numbers and stable action positions.
4. Developer onboarding begins with a deploy-specific, copyable API example. No agent/connector product is introduced.
5. Cost always names currency, unit, period and settlement state. Unknown is never rendered as zero.
6. Public copy requires actual catalog/API evidence. Reference screenshots are research artifacts only, never shipped mock business data.
7. The supplied `benchmark-heatmap.png` is a provider-link strip, **not a benchmark heatmap**. The proposed comparison matrix is an independent design; missing samples remain explicit and every score needs provenance.
8. Review limitations are part of evidence: no private console was accessed, no CAPTCHA bypassed, and unreachable/partially rendered sites contribute no inferred interaction details.
